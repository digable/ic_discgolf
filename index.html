Disc Golf Door

A disc golf door for Synchronet, played here on an emulated 80×25 ANSI screen with the same 3-click meter. It runs the real discgolf.js file, starting at Turkey Creek in Iowa City. Pick your hand and throw, choose a disc, aim with the arrow keys and press SPACE to throw.

Caller's screen · 80×25 · ANSI · CP437telnet · 40 ms

Keys reach the door after the same delay a caller would have, and the screen updates come back the same way.

Throwing1–8 or D picks a disc. T cycles backhand, forehand and hammer. H switches hands (your off hand is weaker), A cycles flat, hyzer and anhyzer. Arrow keys aim.
In the treesYou get the view from your lie automatically. Left/Right picks a line through a gap. P pitches out, R takes PDGA optional relief (+1), V switches between this view and the map.
PuttingInside 66 ft you putt with the same meter. V cycles side, overhead and from-the-player views. Left/Right aims off the basket to play the wind.
